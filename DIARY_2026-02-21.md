# Trading Bot Development Diary - February 21, 2026

## Summary

Today we iterated on the GBP/USD trading bot's AI agents to improve win rate and risk-adjusted returns.

## Starting Point

- **Iter5 Baseline**: 53.6% WR, +10.15 Weighted R
- Three-agent architecture: Direction → Confidence → Levels

## Key Iterations

### Iter6 (Failed - 41.4% WR)
- Made Direction Agent more decisive (removed "most of the time WAIT")
- Added RANGE position-based logic
- **Problem**: Too aggressive, lost the cautious edge

### Iter6.1 (53.3% WR, +12.87 Weighted R)
- Simplified Direction Agent to commit to directions
- Matched baseline win rate, improved Weighted R by 27%

### Iter7 (Failed - 41.4% WR)
- Added mechanical probability caps (RANGE max 0.50, deep pullback max 0.40)
- **Problem**: Mechanical rules don't work - still taking bad trades, just smaller

### Iter7.1 (44.8% WR, +25.70 Normalized R)
- Rewrote Confidence Agent with **intuitive "feel the trade"** approach
- Risk 0 = nothing fits, rules contradict, gambling
- Risk 1 = story makes sense, indicators align
- **Result**: 23% improvement in Normalized R (risk-adjusted returns)
- Lower win rate but better discrimination - sizing down bad trades, sizing up good ones

## Key Insight

The win rate dropped in Iter7.1 because we also changed the Direction Agent. Today's final model combines:
- **Original Direction Agent** (cautious, says WAIT when unclear)
- **Original Levels Agent**
- **New Intuitive Confidence Agent** (feel the trade, 0-1 probability)
- Skip trades with probability < 20%

## Bottlenecks Identified

1. **RANGE Structure**: ~40% of losses from trading in RANGE markets
2. **Deep Pullback (>70%)**: ~25% of losses from exhausted trends
3. **Confidence Clustering**: Old agent gave 55-65% on everything, no discrimination
4. **Stop Loss Too Tight**: Some SL only 3-5 pips, hit by noise
